I wrote down three goals last January: finish more side projects than I start, write
here once a month, and learn enough electronics to stop being scared of a soldering iron.

## What shipped

- **[A Corne keyboard](/projects/hardware/keyboards/corne-build/)**, my first
  through-hole build. I have typed every post since on it.
- **[The weather station](/projects/hardware/weather-station/)**, still logging on the
  back fence.
- **[Tidepool](/projects/software/tidepool/)**, a falling-sand toy that started as a
  weekend experiment with WebAssembly.

## What did not

I wrote nine posts, not twelve. Two drafts about build systems are still sitting in a
folder. One of them will turn into something next year.

## Next year

Fewer goals. Finish [Lantern](/projects/software/lantern/), write when there is something
worth saying, and build a rain gauge that survives autumn.
