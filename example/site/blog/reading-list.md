This page has no date because it is never finished. Newest additions are at the top of
each list.

## On writing

- *On Writing Well* by William Zinsser. Short sentences, strong verbs, no clutter.
- *The Sense of Style* by Steven Pinker. Why the curse of knowledge makes experts write
  badly.

## On building things

- *The Design of Everyday Things* by Don Norman. I reread the chapter on feedback every
  time I design an error message.
- *A Philosophy of Software Design* by John Ousterhout. Deep modules, shallow interfaces.

## On electronics

- *Practical Electronics for Inventors* by Paul Scherz and Simon Monk. The book that got
  me to pick up the soldering iron.
